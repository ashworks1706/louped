---
title: Research domains
description: What loupe can test, organised by question, not by project.
---

A question is an experiment in `experiments/`, named for the question. A system you study comes in
as a model id, an OpenAI-compatible endpoint or logged model calls.

| Domain                      | Capabilities                                                                                                                                                     | Experiments                                                                                                                                            |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Mechanisms                  | directions, residual and per-head patching, logit lens, probes, attention, SAE features, circuits                                                                | `refusal-direction`, `sycophancy-pushback`, `interp-toolkit`, `attention-heads`                                                                        |
| Evaluation                  | grids over seeds with paired intervals and moved/held verdicts, any endpoint as a condition, benchmarks, contamination checks, inference cost, attention kernels | `answer-or-decline`, `benchmarks`, `regression-cases`, `inspect-evals-baseline`, `attention-kernels`, `coding-agent`, `agent-sandbox`, `intercode-ctf` |
| Conditioning                | prompt text, steering vectors, soft prompts, ReFT and LoRA reaching one target                                                                                   | `prompt-conditioning`, `conditioning-methods`                                                                                                          |
| Adapters                    | a bank of LoRA adapters live in any subset, merges, per-site overlap                                                                                             | `diffusion-adapters`                                                                                                                                   |
| Decoding and model families | per-step hooks, adapters switched by phase, masked diffusion models                                                                                              | `diffusion-adapters`                                                                                                                                   |
| Retrieval and grounding     | BM25, dense and hybrid search, reranking; recall, F1, exact match, NLI faithfulness                                                                              | `retrieval-injection`                                                                                                                                  |
| Retrieval inside the model  | passages injected at a layer, retrieval during decoding, spliced KV divergence                                                                                   | `retrieval-injection`, `attention-heads`                                                                                                               |
| Training and data           | logged calls or a teacher to a reviewed set, SFT, DPO, GRPO, tool environments, sweeps                                                                           | `sft-from-traces`, `refusal-finetuning`, `gsm8k-grpo`, `tool-rl`                                                                                       |
