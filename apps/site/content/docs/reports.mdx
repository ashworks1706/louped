`reports/` sits beside `experiments/` and `sources/`. It holds what the project hands to people:
Markdown write-ups, slide decks (`.pptx`), Word documents (`.docx`), PDFs and figures. The
Reports page (G O) lists them and shows each one as it will be read.

## Export a figure

On a run's Figures tab, click SVG, PNG or PDF beside a figure's title. From a terminal or your
agent:

```bash
louped export run:m-3f2/views/umap.json --format png
```

The file goes to `reports/figures/`. Beside it, `<file>.refs.json` keeps the figure's ref and its
trace at the time of export: the run, the script that made the figure and the commit. A Vega-Lite
figure exports through vl-convert. A Plotly figure exports through Kaleido, which draws it in
Chrome; without Chrome, the export stops and says so. The figures the app draws itself, such as
heatmaps and tables, do not export.

The export does not write over a file of the same name, because a deck can show that file. To
write over it, click Replace in the message, or add `--replace` in the terminal. To keep both,
give a new name with `--name`.

## Decks and documents

Your agent writes decks with python-pptx and documents with python-docx, from the run's files and
exported figures. It puts each number's ref beside the number: in a deck, in the slide's speaker
notes; in a document, in the same paragraph or in the paragraph above a table.

The Reports page draws a deck or document with LibreOffice when it is installed, as it prints.
Without LibreOffice, it shows the text of each slide or paragraph and says why.

## Check before you hand it over

`louped check` reads decks and documents in `reports/` as well as Markdown. For a deck it names
the slide, and for a document the paragraph. It also checks that each exported figure's ref still
resolves. A file that it cannot read, such as a damaged deck, is an issue of kind `file`. It
does not stop the check. Office's lock files (`~$deck.pptx`) are skipped. See [Sources](/docs/sources#check-a-write-up) for what it looks for.
