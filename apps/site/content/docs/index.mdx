---
title: Introduction
description: A local-first lab where an intervention on a language model is evaluated, trained against and looked inside.
---

loupe makes an intervention a first-class object. A steered, ablated, adapted or retrieval-augmented
model is one **policy**: an Inspect eval scores it, a paired test says whether it changed anything
beyond seed and sample variance, a trainer can train against it, and the same UI shows what changed
inside the model.

It is glue, not a framework. [nnsight](https://nnsight.net) does interpretability,
[Inspect](https://inspect.aisi.org.uk) does evals and agents, [TRL](https://github.com/huggingface/trl)
does training and [MLflow](https://mlflow.org) does tracking. loupe gives them one model, one
intervention spec and one data format, and a UI over all of it.

Everything runs on your machine with no API keys: open-weight models from the Hugging Face Hub or a
local path, results in files under `LOUPE_HOME`, and no step that calls a hosted model.
