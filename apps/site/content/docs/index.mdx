---
title: Introduction
description: Change a model, prove what changed, and see why.
---

loupe is a local app for research on language model behaviour. You change a model: steer or
ablate a direction, fine-tune it, rewrite its prompt, add an adapter. You prove what changed: the
same evals run on both versions, sample by sample, with paired intervals and a verdict on what
moved and what held. And you see why: logit lens, patching, probes, SAE features and circuits on
the same model under the same change.

A change is one spec, a policy, that runs the same way in an eval, a training run, the Playground
and an analysis. Your question is a short script; loupe supplies the rest and the app to launch and
read it from. It runs on your machine: open-weight models, results in files under `LOUPE_HOME`, no
hosted model and no API key.
