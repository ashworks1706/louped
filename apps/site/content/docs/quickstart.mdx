title: Quick start
description: A result in the app in a few minutes.
---

Every experiment with a `--tiny` mode trains a small model to show the behaviour on your CPU, so
the whole pipeline runs without a download. Its numbers check the pipeline, not the claim.

```sh
just serve   # http://127.0.0.1:8000
```

Everything else happens in the app. Open Launch, pick `refusal-direction/run.py`, tick `--tiny`
and launch. The job's log streams in the page and its runs appear under Runs as they write. The
Playground loads a model from its page.

[Examples](/docs/examples) explains each figure.

## On a real model

Drop `--tiny`. The default, `Qwen/Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct`, runs on a laptop; a GPU makes 1.5B and
up practical.

## Your own question

Press New on the Experiments page: it writes `experiments/my-question/` with a `README.md` and a
`run.py`. The README opens with its domain and status, then states the question, what was
observed, the hypotheses, the baseline, the test, when to stop, and the result. `run.py` writes to MLflow and
Inspect logs, so the app shows it and Launch gets a form for its options.
